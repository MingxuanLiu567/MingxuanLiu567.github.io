About

I am an undergraduate student in Robotics Engineering at Zhejiang Normal University, under the supervision of Prof. Junwu Kan and Prof. Zhonghua Zhang.

My undergraduate research has centered on mechanical systems and piezoelectric vibration energy harvesting, with a particular focus on low-frequency, broadband, and multidirectional energy capture for self-powered sensing. My work spans structural design, mechanical modeling and simulation, prototype development, experimental validation, and data analysis. This research has resulted in four journal papers published in Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing, IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics, and Smart Materials and Structures.

My current research interests lie in embodied intelligence, robot design, and mechatronics. I am particularly interested in exploring how mechanical design, integrated hardware, and intelligent methods can work together to develop robotic systems with greater adaptability, robustness, and autonomy.
